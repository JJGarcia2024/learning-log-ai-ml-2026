## Where a new goal has to land

Every new goal must map to a numbered project (Layer 1, Projects 1–12) or a
BRIDGE-30 Topic (Layer 2, A–F) per `nep-coder`, and to a rung/portfolio slot
on the Extended Study Timeline board. `nep-coder`'s own Phase 1 (Design Doc)
opens with exactly this question — "Which Project (1–12) or BRIDGE-30 Topic
(A–F) does it belong to?" — so don't re-answer it differently here.

- Bad: "improve CFD skills"
- Good: "Does FTCS 1D heat conduction reproduce the analytic linear

## Handoff after this stage

Once the goal is scoped and named, hand off to `nep-coder` for Stages 1–7 —

the goal is scoped and slotted on the Timeline board before nep-coder takes
over. Don't duplicate nep-coder's Phase 1–7 content here or in
`stage-1-plan-spec` through `stage-7-ship`'s aerospace-track files — they
each point back to the matching nep-coder phase instead.
